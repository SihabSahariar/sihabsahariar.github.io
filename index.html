2026 · Windows app · Open source

Umbra

A privacy screen for Windows. It uses the webcam and on-device face tracking to notice when you look away, and hides your screen until you look back. It includes a guided calibration that fits it to each person's desk, four cover styles and a tray app with a global hotkey.

  • MediaPipe
  • OpenCV
  • PyQt5

2026 · Desktop workstation · Open source

ForensicVision

A native desktop workstation for forensic image analysis and enhancement that tells you which parts of a result were measured and which were invented. It covers case management, integrity hashes, AI restoration and super-resolution, comparison views and PDF reports.

  • Image forensics
